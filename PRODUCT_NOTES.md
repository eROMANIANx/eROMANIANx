# PRODUCT_NOTES

## MVP focus

- Excellence on Levels 1-2 (onboarding, watch+quiz, credits, progression).
- Levels 3-5 are scaffolded in schema and messaging as locked/coming soon.

## Configuration map

- Rewards: `reward_rules`
- Progress gates: `level_rules`
- Curated feed: `content_items`
- AI memory and events: `ai_sessions`, `analytics_events`

## Future upgrades

1. Real blockchain wallet support
   - Move from simulated wallet to testnet wallet creation.
   - Add HSM/KMS-backed encryption for private key material.
   - Add chain transaction explorer links.

2. VR/AR communities
   - immersive mentor sessions with moderation and age-safe rooms.
   - skill labs for collaborative simulations.

3. Corporate tracks marketplace
   - sponsor onboarding flow
   - contract templates and outcomes dashboard
   - branded quests and portfolio review workflows

4. Influencer/brand onboarding
   - creator toolkit for publishing short lessons
   - quality scoring and trust badges
   - transparent reward split and policy review

5. Recommendation intelligence
   - ranking service combining interests, completion behavior, and confidence score.
   - explainability panel (“because you watched…”, “because your goal is…”)
